# 🌟 Welcome to Project TRACE: The Ultimate Step-by-Step User Manual

Hello! Welcome to **Project TRACE** (which stands for Tracking, Routing, and Automated Credential Engine).

Please do not worry if you are not good with computers! This manual is written specially for you. It will hold your hand through every single click, button, and screen. We will explain exactly where to look and what to press.

**What is Project TRACE?**
Think of Project TRACE as a digital filing cabinet and delivery system. Instead of handing a piece of paper to someone and hoping it doesn't get lost, you put it into this computer system. The computer then automatically hands it to the right person, and you can always see exactly whose desk it is on.

## 1. Getting Started: How to Log In (For Everyone)

Before you can do anything, you need to "log in." This is like unlocking the front door of the office with your own personal key so the computer knows who you are.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Open your Web Browser:** Look at your computer desktop screen. Find the icon for Google Chrome (a circle with red, yellow, green, and blue), Microsoft Edge (a blue and green swirl), or Safari (a blue compass). Use your mouse to double-click on it.
2. **Go to the Website:** Look at the very top of the window. There is a long white box (the address bar). Click inside it, type the website address given to you by the IT department, and press the **Enter** key on your keyboard.
3. **Look at the Login Screen:** You will see a white box in the middle of your screen asking for your information.
4. **Enter your ID:** Click inside the top text box that says **"STUDENT ID / STAFF ID"**. Type your specific ID number exactly as written below.
5. **Enter your Password:** Click inside the second text box that says **"Password"**. Type the word **`trace2024`** (all lowercase letters, no spaces).
6. **Sign in:** Choose **Login** to submit directly. Staff enter the emailed six-digit code and choose **Verify & Login**; no extra confirmation dialog appears. Both steps show **Processing...** while working and prevent duplicate submissions. Errors appear in the form so you can correct them and retry. Staff need a real, accessible email inbox to receive the code; authentication completes after verification.
7. **Request another code:** If the email has not arrived or the code expired, wait for the 60-second countdown, then choose **Resend OTP**. Use the newest emailed code; a successful resend clears the code you previously typed. Check inline errors if sending fails.

## 2. The Student Guide (Requesting Your Documents)

As a student, you use this system to ask the school for your official papers (like a Transcript or Diploma) without waiting in a long line.

### How to Request a New Document
1. **Log in** using your Student ID (`STU2024001`).
2. **Complete your profile:** If required information is missing, New Request shows a list of missing fields. Choose **Complete Profile** to open Edit Profile, fill in the personal and educational fields, and confirm Save. The progress indicator updates from saved information. Then return to New Request.
3. **Find the New Request button:** Once logged in, look at your main screen (the Dashboard). Look near the top right corner for a prominent button that says **"New Request"**. Click it.
4. **Fill out the form:** A white box (a "modal") will pop up in the middle of your screen.
   - Click on the drop-down menu to pick which document you want.
   - The computer might ask you extra questions. For a Transcript, enter **Year Started** and **Year Ended**. The form shows the rate per printed page and any extra fees. Secretary enters the actual printed pages; your final calculation and total appear on the dashboard afterward.
5. **Upload a picture (if needed):** Some documents require proof, like a picture of your school ID. Look for a large rectangular box with dashed lines around it that says **"Upload"** or **"Drop files here"**.
   - Click inside that dashed box.
   - A window showing the files on your computer will open. Find the picture of your ID, click it once, and then click the **"Open"** button at the bottom right of that window.
6. **Submit:** Look at the bottom right corner of the popup box. Click the green button that says **"Submit Request"**.
   - *Note: You do not pay yet! The school needs to calculate the exact price first.*

### How to Track Your Document
1. Look at the middle of your screen on your Dashboard. You will see a list with the heading **"Active Requests"**. This is a list of all the papers you have asked for.
2. Click anywhere on one of your requests in that list.
3. A visual timeline (the **Live Tracker**) will appear on your screen. It looks like a line with dots.
   - The dot that is highlighted in color shows you exactly where your paper is right now (for example, "Pending Intake" means the front desk is looking at it, "Pending Secretary Evaluation" means the secretary is reviewing it).

### How to Pay Your Bill
When the Secretary finishes pricing all documents in a request, its payment action becomes available.
1. Look at the very top of your dashboard screen. You will see a large, brightly colored banner that says **"Action Required - Payment"**.
2. Each ready request has one button showing its combined total and document count, such as **"Pay ₱200.00 (2 documents)"**. Review the document amounts listed beneath it, then click that button. Separate requests have separate payment buttons.
3. A new window will pop up. Click on the circle next to how you want to pay (like GCash, Bank Transfer, or Over-the-Counter).
4. Follow the instructions on the screen. If you chose an online method like GCash, there will be a dashed box. Click it to upload a picture of your GCash receipt, just like you did for your ID earlier.
5. Find the box labeled **"Reference Number"**. Click it and type the long string of numbers from your GCash receipt.
6. Check the request, document count, total, and breakdown, then click **"Submit Payment"**. Read the confirmation and choose **Confirm Payment** to send it. Cancel keeps the reference and attachment. One receipt covers every document in that request.

The Active Requests table shows progress and status for each document. Use **Live Track** to inspect its progress, or **Cancel** when cancellation is available. At narrow widths, scroll within the table card to reach the later columns.

## 3. The Window 1 Clerk Guide (Intake & Release)

As the Window 1 Clerk (`WINDOW1001`), you are the front door. You check requests when they first arrive, and you hand the printed papers to the students at the very end.

### Looking at Your Dashboard Tabs
The dashboard has an upload card beside one workspace with **Intake** and **Release** queue tabs. On narrow screens these stack vertically. **Tracking Desk** remains a separate sidebar destination.

### Task 1: The Intake Queue (Checking New Requests)
1. In the dashboard's **Intake Queue**, choose **Check** beside the request.
2. Review the student details and any supporting paperwork. Attachment requirements depend on the document type.
3. If correct, choose **Route to Secretary**, then confirm.
4. For a correction, enter the reason in **Notes**, choose **Return to Student**, then confirm. Notes are available even for documents without a required attachment. Cancelling keeps the notes; a return leaves the request at intake with the correction message.
5. For a walk-in, choose **Manual Entry**, fill the form, choose **Submit Request**, then confirm. The form resets only after a successful submission.

### Task 2: The Release Queue (Giving Documents to Students)
1. Find the student in the dashboard's **Release Desk**. These documents have passed the Secretary's OR check and physical handoff.
2. Compare the recorded OR number with the physical receipt. **View** is available when Finance has uploaded its copy; **Digital copy pending upload** means the retained copy will be added later.
3. Choose **Release Doc**, review the confirmation, then choose **Release** once the physical handover is complete. Cancel records nothing.
4. A missing digital copy does not block release. There is no release-stage return-to-Secretary action in the current system; resolve a physical receipt discrepancy with the desks before confirming release.

### Task 3: The Tracking Desk (Answering Questions)
1. If a student walks up and asks, "Where is my document?", click the **Tracking Desk** tab.
2. Look for the white search box that says "Search". Click inside it and type the student's name.
3. Press **Enter** on your keyboard. The screen will instantly show you exactly whose desk their paper is sitting on, so you can tell the student.

## 4. The College Secretary Guide (Evaluation & Pricing)

As a College Secretary (e.g., `SEC-CCS001`), you do the actual printing and signing for the students in your specific college.

### Looking at Your Dashboard Tabs
The four queue tabs are **Initial Evaluation**, **Processing & Pricing**, **OR Verification**, and **Final Handoff**. Each shows its queue count; only the selected queue table is displayed.

### Task 1: Evaluation Queue (Reviewing the Request)
1. Click the **Evaluation Queue** tab. Click on a document in the list to open it.
2. Your screen will split into two halves.
   - On the **left side**, you will see the document or ID the student uploaded.
   - On the **right side**, you will see boxes with the student's information.
3. Read the left side. Compare it to the right side.
4. If it looks correct, look at the bottom right. Click the button that says **"Accept"**.
5. A small box will pop up asking for an **"Estimated Ready Date"**. Click the little calendar icon next to the box, and click on the date you think the document will be printed and ready. (This is very important, as it texts the student so they know when to expect it!). Click **Save** or **Confirm**.

### Task 2: Processing & Pricing (Doing the Work)
1. Prepare, print, sign, and seal the document.
2. In **Processing & Pricing**, choose **Set Price** or **Adjust Price**.
3. Enter the amount and **Pages printed**. The current pricing form has no notes field.
4. Choose **Save Price** or **Save & Bill Student**, then confirm **Save Price**. Billing occurs when the last document in the request has a price; the payment slip opens then. Otherwise the pricing dialog closes.

### Task 3: OR Verification and Final Handoff
1. In **OR Verification**, choose **Verify Receipt**.
2. Check the OR number against the physical Official Receipt Finance handed over, or inspect its uploaded copy.
3. When the digital copy is pending, select **I inspected the physical Official Receipt…** after checking it. An OR number is required. Choose **Confirm Receipt** to record the check; Cancel records nothing.
4. The physical inspection is recorded in the audit notes. Finance can upload its retained copy later without delaying the document.
5. Physically pass the printed document to Window 1, then use **Final Handoff** and confirm the handoff. Receipt verification and handoff are separate actions.

## 5. The Finance Clerk Guide (Payments)

As the Finance Clerk (`FINANCE001`), you handle the money. You check bank transfers and hand out Official Receipts (OR).

### Looking at Your Dashboard Tabs
The queue tabs are **Awaiting Payment**, **Verification Queue**, and **Transactions & OR Copies**, each with its current count.

### Task 1: Awaiting Payment (For Walk-In Students)
1. Click the **Awaiting Payment** tab. This shows a list of everyone who owes money but hasn't paid yet.
2. If a student walks into your office and hands you physical cash, find their name in this list.
3. Click on their name. You will see exactly how much money they owe.
4. Take their money, and write them a paper Official Receipt (OR) from your receipt book.
5. Look at the computer screen. Click the button that says **"Log Counter Payment"**.
6. Enter the **OR Number**, receipt date, and any notes. The billed total and document breakdown are shown for checking. A retained receipt copy is optional: selecting it stays local; choose **Read Receipt** only if you want OCR assistance, then check the extracted values.
7. Choose the form's save action, review the confirmation, then confirm. Logging a payment still requires Finance verification.

### Task 2: Verification Queue (For Online Payments like GCash)
1. Click the **Verification Queue** tab. This is for students who paid online from their phones.
2. Click on a payment in the list. The screen will show you a picture of their phone screenshot (like a GCash text message).
3. Open a new window on your computer or look at your school's official mobile phone to check the school's actual bank account.
4. **Compare:** Look at the "Reference Number" the student typed on the screen, and see if that exact same number is in the school's real bank account.
5. **If the money is really there:** Click the box labeled **"Official Receipt (OR) Number"**, type in the OR number you are assigning to this payment, and choose **Verify Payment**, then confirm **Verify Payment**. The digital OR attachment is optional. Hand the physical Official Receipt to the College Secretary; Finance can upload its retained second copy later.
6. **If the money is NOT there (Error Handling):** What if the screenshot is fake or blurry?
   - Find the large text box labeled **"Clerk Notes"**. Click inside it and type clearly what is wrong (e.g., "The reference number does not match our records. Please try again.").
   - Click the red button that says **"Reject Payment"**. The computer will send your note directly to the student so they can fix it.

### Task 3: Upload the Retained OR Copy Later
1. Open **Transactions & OR Copies**, which includes paid requests through completion.
2. Find a row marked **Digital copy pending upload** and choose **Upload OR**.
3. Select the retained receipt image or PDF, choose **Upload Receipt**, then **Confirm Upload**.
4. Cancel or a failed upload keeps the selected file. A successful upload adds the copy to the existing request group without changing payment status or the document's stage.

## 6. The Registrar Admin Guide (Overseer)

As the Registrar Admin (`ADMIN001`), you are the boss of the system. You don't process the papers yourself; you watch the system to make sure it runs smoothly.

### Task 1: Watching the Dashboard
The **System Throughput** card shows the average processing duration for completed requests, with its unit. Its **Daily completed documents** chart shows counts, not processing time. Hover over a point, or focus the chart with Tab and use Left/Right arrows, to read the date and document count. If there are no completed requests, the card says **No completed requests yet** and shows no illustrative completion curve.

1. Log in. Your main screen is your command center.
2. **AI Insights:** Look at the middle of the screen. The computer will put warning messages here. For example, if it says "Window 1 is backed up," that means you should probably send someone to help Window 1 because they have too much work.
3. **Volume Forecast:** Look at the chart (the graph with lines). The computer looks at past data to guess how busy the office will be next week. If the line goes up very high on Tuesday, you know to schedule extra staff on Tuesday.

### Task 2: System Maintenance (Adding new staff or changing prices)
1. Look at the very left edge of your screen. There is a dark menu bar running from top to bottom. Click on the words **"System Maintenance"**.
2. **Accounts:** Open **System Maintenance → Accounts** for students, alumni, and staff. Open a card, choose **Edit User**, review the supported name, email, phone, college, and program fields, then confirm Save. IDs are read-only. **Add User**, temporary-password changes, and activation controls retain their staff-only permissions.
3. **To Change Prices:** Open **Document Types**, choose **Edit** on a row, change its fee or rules, then **Save Changes → Save** in the confirmation. Cancel keeps the draft.
4. **Payment Settings:** Click **"Manage Payment Methods"**. If the school's bank account number changes, you click here, erase the old account number, type the new one, and click save. This updates what the students see when they pay.

### Task 3: Reports & Analytics (Printing Records)
1. Look at the left menu bar again. Click on **"Reports"**.
2. Apply the report filters, select **Document Report CSV** or a student export category from **Export options**, then choose **Export**. Document CSV uses the current filters. The on-screen dates use Philippine time and amounts use pesos.
3. Click on the **"Efficiency Analytics"** tab at the top. Here, the computer tells you how fast your staff is working. It will literally say things like "Average time for Intake: 2 hours." If you see a number that is way too slow (like 14 days), you know you need to talk to that department to see why they are struggling.

After checking the staff identity, give the code directly to that person. Save/download the recovery codes shown once. Later logins use their app independently of Admin email. This initial setup cannot replace an already-enrolled factor; contact Registrar for identity-verified recovery if the phone and recovery codes are lost.
